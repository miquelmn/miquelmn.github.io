Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become a highly important technology due to its endless possibilities for improving current computer applications. In fact, AI algorithms are integrated into the infrastructure of many search engines in commercial products, industrial applications, medical diagnostics, voice or object recognition applications, robot control, or video games.

This course offers a broad overview of modern artificial intelligence, and its main objective is for students to understand how machines can participate in problem-solving, and even how they can learn to solve them autonomously. The methodology used will be based on explaining the main AI algorithms and testing them practically through exercises and applications that will be carried out throughout the course.
